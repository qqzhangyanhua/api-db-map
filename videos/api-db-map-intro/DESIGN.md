## Style Prompt

Clean technical engineering infographic for a multi-skill AI agent system. Dark navy command-center canvas, precise routing diagrams, modular skill nodes, crisp lines, restrained cinematic contrast. The tone is calm, direct, and architectural, matching a Chinese engineering article about routing, priority, conflict resolution, and fallback.

## Colors

- `#07111f` background: deep navy canvas
- `#0f2236` panel: elevated technical surfaces
- `#37d5ff` cyan: routing and active signal
- `#ffbe55` amber: priority and warning
- `#ff5a67` red: conflicts and risk
- `#5ce38a` green: stable resolution and fallback
- `#e8f1ff` text: primary readable text

## Typography

- Primary: `Inter`, `Noto Sans SC`, system sans-serif
- Headings: heavy weight, compact line-height, no negative letter spacing
- Body: medium weight, short lines for mobile-readable video capture

## What NOT to Do

- No generic blue-purple gradients.
- No decorative blobs, orbs, bokeh, or stock-like backgrounds.
- No dense paragraphs copied from the article.
- No tiny labels that require pausing to read.
- No floating card stacks inside other cards.
